# M7b — the second model-planner run, 2026-10-30

Status: record of one evaluation run. It holds aggregate figures, case IDs from the
committed gold file and public digests only (the M7b data rules:
[`m7b-gold-labels.md`](m7b-gold-labels.md#what-is-committed)); the evidence sets, the
model replies and the report stay in the operator's local result directory.

What the run shows: after the work packages of
[`m7b-work-packages.md`](m7b-work-packages.md) (D, A1, A2, B, the round-1 skip, C and its
mixed-script follow-up), the model planner's plans all pass the gate and the calls are
fast and stable. It does not show that the model planner improves an investigation: its
coverage equals the deterministic policy's within noise, and where it differs, the
difference comes from the model leaving words out of its searches and from how B's
evidence needs judge that.

- The deterministic run's 4 baseline wrong stops are 20 in five runs; the model's 19 is
  not a quality gain. The one fewer is a `5g-aka-implementations` run that ended
  `budget_exhausted`, not one whose evidence became sufficient.
- The one baseline `false_gap` is a `suci-deconcealment` run whose answer the gold judges
  sufficient but which ended `budget_exhausted`.
- `kamailio-version-affected` answers differ from the deterministic run in all five runs,
  yet its mean coverage rises from 0.95 to 0.99; differing answers are not a regression
  by themselves.
- Holdout: `wrong_stop` 0/20 does not mean the holdout passes. `nas-threats` reports its
  sufficient answer as a gap in all five runs (`false_gap` 5/10), because the direct FiGHT
  search warns `term_not_indexed` for `signalling` (C1b's spelling sensitivity). The four
  holdout requests count as seen and were not used for tuning
  ([`m7b-work-packages.md`](m7b-work-packages.md)).

## Causes

**The model leaves words out of its round-1 search.** In the differing runs the model's
`cve_search` sends fewer of the question's search terms than the deterministic plan does
(for `kamailio-version-affected`, between 2 and 6 of 7). The gate accepts that: a search
may use any subset of the question's vocabulary. B then judges the implementation need
`found` only when the admitted searches together sent every search term
([`m7b-b-evidence-needs.md`](m7b-b-evidence-needs.md)), so the need stays open and the run
goes to round 2, which the deterministic runs of these cases never reach.

**Round 2 helps unevenly.** Of the 10 round-2 runs, 4 end with the implementation need
`found`: one `ueransim` and one `suci-deconcealment` run end `sufficient`, one
`kamailio-version-affected` run ends `source_unavailable` (the need met, affectedness
still unserved), and one `suci-deconcealment` run meets the need but still ends
`budget_exhausted`. The other 6 end `needs_unmet` or `budget_exhausted` with the need not
found. Every `budget_exhausted` ending in this run is a breach of the context-bytes
budget, reached after a third CVE search or, in the `5g-aka-implementations` run, after a
model-added search beside the topic call. So the extra round is not useless, but its gain is not
stable, and it can keep spending budget after the answer is already sufficient.

## Limits

- One run of each planner on one store and one FiGHT snapshot; 13 baseline and 4 holdout
  cases.
- The gold labels judge the listed items; they say nothing about evidence a run did not
  retrieve.
- The quality gaps recorded in `m7b-work-packages.md` stay open: `wrong_stop` 4/13 for the
  deterministic policy, 7 protocol items judged irrelevant, and C1b's dependence on
  spelling.

## What follows

The owner decided on the product policy for free text: a question with no kept CVE ID and
no resolved topic is planned by the deterministic policy in every scope, and a configured
model is not consulted for it (ADR 0005 §3, contracts `2.0.0-dev.32`, policy `m7b.3`
unchanged). The policy's search of every question term is its baseline, not a
completeness or sufficiency guarantee: sending every term does not make every result
match every term, nor find every relevant CVE. `gtp-mobile-core` and `oai-cn5g` are
free-text runs that sent every term, yet stop `sufficient` wrongly.

- 9 of the 13 baseline cases are free text (`open5gs-amf`, `kamailio-version-affected`,
  `ueransim`, `srsran-negative`, `free5gc`, `oai-cn5g`, `suci-deconcealment`,
  `gtp-mobile-core`, `sip-ims-threats`). Their model runs repeat the deterministic
  run, so their agreement with it is a routing check, not a model-quality result. Only
  the 4 topic and exact-ID cases (`5g-aka-threats`, `5g-aka-implementations`,
  `named-cve-mixed`, `open5gs-named-affected`) say anything about the model. Report
  format 5 leaves the policy's `model_not_consulted` rounds out of the model rates.
- `kamailio-version-affected`'s observed gain (mean coverage 0.95 to 0.99 over five
  runs, from the model's searches of fewer words) is given up. It was never shown to be
  stable, and nothing yet says when leaving words out helps. That does not show that the
  model has no value; query rewriting by a model stays a research direction that
  evaluation may reopen.
- Answers that differ from the deterministic run's, with little overlap in the listed
  top 20, show that the ranking changed, not that quality fell; saying more needs a gold
  or independent relevance judgement. Equal required coverage does not show that
  precision or noise are unchanged either.
- The model can still search a subset of the vocabulary in topic and exact-ID rounds,
  and the need judgement (every term sent) still differs there from the answer merge
  (the first candidate seen). These stay open for the next analysis.
- The evidence sets behind this report are `2.0.0-dev.31`; the dev.32 code refuses that
  schema version, so they are verified and replayed at commit `1dbb4a1`.
